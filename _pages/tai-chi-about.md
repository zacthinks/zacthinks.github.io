I began practicing Tai Chi as a graduate student, initially as a way to create some balance with the very cerebral work of academic life. What started as a counterweight to research gradually became a serious and enduring part of my life.

## Training and lineage

I am primarily trained in Yang-style Tai Chi and am a seventh-generation practitioner. I trained with my teacher, **Yu Luyun**, in Taiwan.

## Teaching

I taught Tai Chi at the Cambridge YMCA for several years, often alongside my mother. We worked with students ranging from complete beginners to longer-term practitioners. Since I graduated, some of our former students have continued the classes, which remain a good option for people looking for a more structured weekly practice.
